PROJECT OVERVIEW
Project Name: Riverside Residential Development - Phase 2
Project Location: Reading, Berkshire, UK
Contract Duration: 18 months
Estimated Contract Value: £4.5 million - £5.2 million

SCOPE OF WORK
Supply and delivery of construction materials for 150-unit residential development:

1. STRUCTURAL MATERIALS
   - Concrete: 2,500 m³ ready-mix concrete (C30/37 grade)
   - Steel reinforcement bars: 450 tonnes (various diameters 12mm-32mm)
   - Structural steel sections: 180 tonnes (I-beams, H-columns)
   - Precast concrete elements: 850 units (lintels, stairs, panels)

2. MASONRY & BRICKWORK
   - Facing bricks: 850,000 units (red multi stock, frost-resistant)
   - Engineering bricks: 120,000 units (Class B)
   - Blocks: 180,000 units (100mm, 140mm lightweight aggregate)
   - Mortar: 450 tonnes (various mixes)

3. ROOFING MATERIALS
   - Roof tiles: 45,000 units (interlocking concrete tiles, slate grey)
   - Roof underlay: 8,500 m²
   - Guttering & downpipes: Complete system for 150 units (uPVC, anthracite grey)
   - Roof insulation: 9,500 m² (300mm mineral wool, U-value 0.16)

4. TIMBER & JOINERY
   - Structural timber: 185 m³ (treated softwood, C24 grade)
   - Floor joists: 2,850 linear meters (various sizes)
   - Roof trusses: 150 sets (pre-fabricated to specification)
   - Window frames: 600 units (uPVC, white, double-glazed, A-rated)
   - Door sets: 450 units (internal) + 150 units (external, composite)

5. INSULATION & DAMP PROOFING
   - Wall insulation: 12,000 m² (cavity wall insulation batts)
   - Floor insulation: 8,500 m² (100mm rigid PIR boards)
   - Damp-proof membrane: 9,000 m² (1200 gauge)
   - Damp-proof course: 2,500 linear meters

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- All materials must comply with UK Building Regulations 2010 (as amended)
- CE marking required for regulated products
- UKCA marking accepted where applicable
- Materials must meet minimum environmental standards (BRE Green Guide A or B rating)
- Chain of custody certification required for timber products (FSC or PEFC)
- Fire performance: Materials for residential use must achieve minimum Class 1 surface spread

DELIVERY REQUIREMENTS
- Delivery Schedule: Phased deliveries over 18 months as per construction program
- Delivery Location: Site compound, Riverside Development, Reading RG2 5XX
- Access: HGV access available via dedicated site entrance
- Storage: Limited on-site storage; JIT delivery preferred
- Packaging: Returnable packaging systems preferred; waste minimization required

QUALITY & CERTIFICATION
- ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management certification (mandatory)
- ISO 14001:2015 Environmental Management (preferred)
- ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health & Safety (preferred)
- Product test certificates and compliance documentation required
- Material traceability system required

SUSTAINABILITY REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum 25% recycled content where feasible
- EPD (Environmental Product Declaration) preferred
- Local sourcing preferred (within 250km radius)
- Carbon footprint calculation required for major materials
- Waste management plan required
- Circular economy approach encouraged

PRICING REQUIREMENTS
Suppliers must provide:
- Unit prices for each material category
- Volume discounts if applicable
- Delivery costs (separate line item)
- Price validity period (minimum 90 days)
- Payment terms (specify early payment discounts if available)
- Price variation clauses (if any)

EVALUATION CRITERIA
Proposals will be evaluated on the following weighted criteria:
1. Price (40%)
   - Competitive pricing
   - Total cost of ownership
   - Payment terms

2. Technical Capability (30%)
   - Product quality and specifications
   - Compliance with standards
   - Technical support

3. Delivery & Logistics (15%)
   - Delivery reliability track record
   - Flexibility in scheduling
   - Stock availability

4. Sustainability (10%)
   - Environmental credentials
   - Carbon footprint
   - Circular economy initiatives

5. Experience & References (5%)
   - Previous similar projects
   - Client references
   - Financial stability

SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
Suppliers must submit:
1. Company profile and registration documents
2. Financial statements (last 2 years)
3. Insurance certificates (Public Liability £10M, Professional Indemnity £5M)
4. Health & Safety policy and accident statistics
5. Environmental policy and certifications
6. Quality management system documentation
7. Detailed technical specifications for all materials
8. Pricing schedule (using provided template)
9. Delivery schedule and logistics plan
10. References from 3 similar projects (last 3 years)
11. Samples of key materials (bricks, tiles, timber)

CONTRACTUAL TERMS
- Contract Type: Fixed price with limited variation clauses
- Payment Terms: Net 30 days from invoice date
- Retention: 5% until practical completion
- Warranty: Minimum 12 months from delivery
- Liquidated Damages: £5,000 per day for late delivery (capped at 10% contract value)
- Insurance: Comprehensive coverage required
