DEVELOPMENT PROPOSAL: RIVERSIDE TOWERS - MIXED-USE RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Name: Riverside Towers
Address: 1250 Waterfront Boulevard, Seattle, WA 98101
Applicant: Riverside Development LLC
Architect: Pacific Design Associates
Developer: Urban Living Communities
Planning Consultant: Seattle Planning Services Inc.

2. DEVELOPMENT DESCRIPTION

This application seeks planning permission for the construction of a mixed-use residential development comprising two towers on a 2.5-acre waterfront site. The development will provide much-needed housing in the downtown core while activating the riverfront with public amenities and commercial spaces.

3. PROPOSED DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATIONS

Building Configuration:
- Tower A (North): 28 storeys
- Tower B (South): 32 storeys
- Podium: 5 storeys (shared base for both towers)
- Total Height: Tower A - 295 feet, Tower B - 340 feet

Gross Floor Area (GFA):
- Residential: 485,000 sq.ft
- Commercial/Retail: 45,000 sq.ft
- Community Amenity Space: 15,000 sq.ft
- Total GFA: 545,000 sq.ft

Site Area: 108,900 sq.ft (2.5 acres)

4. RESIDENTIAL UNIT BREAKDOWN

Total Units: 425 residential units

Unit Type Distribution:
- Studio Units: 75 (18%)
- One-Bedroom Units: 180 (42%)
- Two-Bedroom Units: 135 (32%)
- Three-Bedroom Units: 35 (8%)

Affordable Housing Component:
- 85 units (20%) designated as affordable housing
- Income levels: 60-80% Area Median Income (AMI)
- Mix of studio, 1BR, and 2BR units

5. COMMERCIAL AND AMENITY SPACES

Residential Amenities:

6. PARKING AND TRANSPORTATION

Parking Provisions:

Riverside Towers represents a high-quality, mixed-use residential development that aligns with Seattle's planning objectives for downtown intensification, affordable housing provision, and waterfront activation.
