## Getting Started

### What is BidRadar?

BidRadar is an AI-powered platform that helps businesses discover, analyze, and track public sector procurement opportunities. Think of it as your intelligent radar system for tender opportunities - automatically detecting relevant contracts, analyzing requirements, and alerting you to the best matches for your business.

### Who Should Use BidRadar?

- **SME Suppliers**: Small and medium enterprises seeking public sector contracts
- **Service Providers**: Companies offering IT, consulting, facilities management, or professional services
- **Business Development Teams**: Teams responsible for identifying new business opportunities
- **Procurement Consultants**: Advisors helping clients find and win tenders
- **Start-ups**: New businesses looking for their first public sector contracts

### Key Benefits

1. **Save Time**: 90% reduction in tender search time through automation
2. **Never Miss Opportunities**: Daily automated scanning of procurement portals
3. **Get Personalized Matches**: AI recommends tenders that fit your capabilities
4. **Understand Requirements**: AI Assistant analyzes tender documents and compliance needs
5. **Track the Market**: Real-time analytics on procurement trends and competition

### Example Conversation Flow

**Scenario**: Understanding a specific tender

```
You: Tell me about the Cloud Computing Services framework tender

AI: The Framework Agreement for Cloud Computing Services (LUPC-2024-001)
    is a £2.5M opportunity closing in 45 days. Key points:

    - Scope: IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS solutions for UK universities
    - Evaluation: Likely quality/price split
    - Requirements: Cloud security, scalability, UK data residency
    - Suitable for: Established cloud providers with education sector experience

    Would you like me to analyze the compliance requirements or
    evaluation criteria in more detail?

You: What are the compliance requirements?

AI: [Switch to Compliance Helper mode automatically]

    Key compliance requirements for this tender likely include:

    1. Mandatory Certifications:
       - ISO 27001 (Information Security)
       - Cyber Essentials Plus
       - G-Cloud framework listing (preferred)

    2. Technical Standards:
       - UK GDPR compliance
       - Data sovereignty (UK-based data centers)
       - Industry-standard SLAs (99.9% uptime minimum)

    3. Financial Standing:
       - Minimum £2M annual turnover
       - Professional indemnity insurance (£5M+)
       - Financial stability documentation

    4. Social Value:
       - Environmental sustainability commitments
       - Support for skills development
       - Diversity and inclusion policies

    Would you like guidance on any specific requirement?
```

### Tips for Effective AI Interaction

**Be Specific**:
- ❌ "Tell me about tenders"
- ✅ "What are the key requirements for IT service tenders over £500k?"

**Provide Context**:
- ❌ "Is this a good opportunity?"
- ✅ "My company specializes in cloud migrations with 10 years experience and £5M turnover. Is tender LUPC-2024-001 a good fit?"

**Ask Follow-ups**:
- Use the conversation history
- "Can you elaborate on point 3?"
- "What about smaller companies?"

**Use the Right Mode**:
- Switch modes for different types of questions
- Don't use Market Research for compliance questions

**💡 Pro Tip**: Treat the AI like a consultant. The more context you provide, the better the advice.

### Limitations

**What the AI CAN do**:
- Analyze publicly available tender information
- Provide general procurement guidance
- Explain regulations and best practices
- Offer strategic insights based on market data

**What the AI CANNOT do**:
- Access confidential tender documents
- Provide legal advice (consult a lawyer)
- Guarantee tender wins
- Make bid/no-bid decisions for you
- Submit tenders on your behalf

## Tender Recommendations

Navigate to **🎯 Recommendations** from the sidebar.

The Recommendations engine uses machine learning to match tenders to your business profile.

### How Recommendations Work

The system analyzes four key dimensions:

1. **Category Match (40%)**: How well the tender category aligns with your business sectors
2. **Content Similarity (30%)**: Semantic similarity between tender text and your capabilities
3. **Value Alignment (20%)**: Whether contract value fits your preferences
4. **Preferences (10%)**: Location, timeline, and other preferences

Each tender receives:
- **Match Score**: 0-100% overall match quality
- **Match Reasons**: Specific reasons for the recommendation
- **Confidence Level**: How certain the system is about the match

### Setting Up Your Profile

Before getting recommendations, you need a vendor profile:

1. Navigate to **👤 Vendor Dashboard**
2. If no profile exists, fill out the registration form:

**Company Information**:
- Company Name (required)
- Contact Person
- Email Address (required)
- Phone Number
- Website

**Business Profile**:
- Company Size: Small (10-49), Medium (50-249), Large (250+)
- Years of Experience
- Annual Turnover: Select range
- Certifications: ISO, industry-specific accreditations

**Business Categories** (required):
- Select all categories that apply to your business
- Examples: IT Services & Software, Professional Services, etc.
- More categories = more diverse recommendations

**Capability Keywords** (required):
- Comma-separated keywords describing your expertise
- Examples: "cloud computing, cybersecurity, software development, DevOps"
- Be specific but comprehensive
- These are used for semantic matching

**Address**:
- Street Address
- City
- Postcode
- Country

**Tender Preferences**:
- Minimum Contract Value: Smallest contract you'd bid for
- Maximum Contract Value: Largest contract you can handle
- Preferred Regions: Where you're willing to work (multi-select)

**Notification Settings**:
- Email Notifications: Yes/No
- Frequency: Daily, Weekly, Immediate, Custom

**💡 Pro Tip**: Be honest about your capabilities. Better to get 10 high-quality matches than 100 irrelevant ones.

### Viewing Recommendations

1. Ensure you have a vendor profile (see above)
2. Navigate to **🎯 Recommendations**
3. Select your company from the dropdown
4. Click **"Get Recommendations"**
5. System analyzes all tenders and generates personalized matches

### Understanding Recommendation Results

#### Top Section: Summary

- **Recommendations Found**: Total number of matches
- **High Match (70%+)**: Very strong fits
- **Medium Match (40-69%)**: Good potential fits
- **Average Match Score**: Overall quality of recommendations

#### Recommendation Cards

Each recommended tender shows:

**Header**:
- Tender title
- Match percentage (color-coded: 🟢 70%+, 🟡 40-69%, 🔴 <40%)

**Key Information**:
- Category
- Contract Value
- Deadline
- Status
- Organization

**Match Analysis**:
- **Match Score**: Overall percentage
- **Confidence**: How reliable the match is (based on how many algorithms agreed)
- **Match Reasons**: Specific bullet points explaining why this tender matches your profile

Example match reasons:
- "Direct category match: IT Services & Software"
- "Content similarity: 0.85"
- "Value £480,000 within preferred range"
- "Location match: UK"
- "Adequate preparation time"

**Actions**:
- **👍 Interested**: Mark as interesting (tracked for ML improvement)
- **👎 Not Relevant**: Mark as not relevant (helps refine future recommendations)
- **View Details**: See full tender information

### Improving Recommendations

The system learns from your feedback:

**Mark Interests**:
- Click 👍 on tenders you like
- Click 👎 on poor matches
- This trains the algorithm over time

**Update Your Profile**:
- Add new capabilities as you grow
- Update value ranges as capacity increases
- Refine keywords based on what works

**Provide Feedback**:
- Use the feedback form at the bottom
- Tell us if recommendations are improving
- Suggest categories or features

**💡 Pro Tip**: Review recommendations weekly. Mark 5-10 tenders each time to rapidly improve accuracy.

### Using Recommendations Strategically

**Immediate Action** (High Match 70%+):
- Review these first
- Typically 5-10 tenders
- Highest probability of success
- Add to bid pipeline

**Medium Priority** (Medium Match 40-69%):
- Review if capacity available
- May require more effort to be competitive
- Good for market intelligence
- Consider for future capability development

**Market Intelligence** (Low Match <40%):
- Don't ignore completely
- Shows market trends
- Identifies adjacent opportunities
- May indicate new capabilities to develop

**Pipeline Management**:
1. Export high-match tenders weekly
2. Add to your bid calendar
3. Track deadlines in your CRM
4. Allocate resources based on match score

## Alert Center

Navigate to **🔔 Alert Center** from the sidebar.

### Understanding Alerts

Alerts automatically notify you when new tenders match your vendor profile.

**How Alerts Work**:
1. System scrapes new tenders daily
2. Runs your vendor profile against new opportunities
3. Generates alerts for matches above threshold (typically 40%+)
4. Delivers via your preferred method (email, in-app, or both)

### Alert Types

**High Priority** (70%+ match):
- Strong profile alignment
- Immediate attention recommended
- Icon: 🔴 or "High Priority" label

**Medium Priority** (40-69% match):
- Good potential fit
- Review when capacity available
- Icon: 🟡 or "Medium Priority" label

**New Tender** (any new tender in your categories):
- Informational
- Not necessarily high match
- Icon: 🔵 or "New" label

### Managing Alerts

**Alert Dashboard**:
- Unread count at top
- Sortable by date, priority, category
- Filter by status: Unread, Read, Dismissed

**Alert Actions**:
- **Mark as Read**: Acknowledge the alert
- **View Tender**: Go to tender details
- **Dismiss**: Remove from active alerts
- **Interested**: Add to tracking and mark interest

**Batch Actions**:
- Select multiple alerts (checkboxes)
- Mark all as read
- Dismiss selected
- Export selected

### Notification Preferences

Configure in Vendor Profile:

**Email Notifications**:
- On/Off toggle
- Uses email from vendor profile
- Includes daily digest option

**Frequency**:
- **Immediate**: Email within minutes of new match
- **Daily**: Morning digest (recommended)
- **Weekly**: Monday morning summary
- **Custom**: Specify days/times

**Threshold**:
- Only alert for matches above certain score
- Default: 40%
- Recommended: 50-60% to reduce noise

**💡 Pro Tip**: Start with daily digest. Adjust frequency based on opportunity volume in your sector.

### Email Alert Format

Daily Digest includes:
- Subject: "BidRadar: X new tender opportunities for [Your Company]"
- Summary: High/medium priority count
- Top 3 Matches: Title, value, deadline, match score, link
- Link to full alert center
- Manage preferences link

### Alert Best Practices

**Daily Routine**:
1. Check alerts first thing in the morning
2. Review high priority immediately
3. Medium priority when time permits
4. Dismiss irrelevant alerts

**Weekly Routine**:
1. Review alert patterns (which categories most frequent?)
2. Update profile if getting poor matches
3. Export interested tenders to bid pipeline
4. Clear dismissed alerts

**Monthly Routine**:
1. Analyze which alerts led to bid activity
2. Adjust match threshold if needed
3. Update notification preferences
4. Review missed opportunities

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### No Recommendations Appearing

**Possible Causes**:
- No vendor profile created
- Profile too narrow (very specific categories + tight value range)
- No tenders in database matching your criteria

**Solutions**:
1. Check if profile exists (Vendor Dashboard)
2. Broaden categories (add 1-2 more)
3. Widen value range by 25%
4. Refresh tender data (may be outdated)

#### Low Match Scores

**Possible Causes**:
- Keywords don't align with tender language
- Categories too broad or too narrow
- Value preferences misaligned with market

**Solutions**:
1. Review actual tender text in your category
2. Update keywords to match industry terminology
3. Try natural language search to see what tenders exist
4. Adjust value range based on market reality

#### AI Assistant Not Responding

**Possible Causes**:
- API key not configured
- Network connectivity issues
- Service temporarily unavailable

**Solutions**:
1. Check error message for specific issue
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Check internet connection
4. Wait 5 minutes and retry
5. Use basic search as fallback

#### Search Returns No Results

**Possible Causes**:
- Query too specific
- No tenders matching criteria
- Spelling errors in query

**Solutions**:
1. Simplify query (remove one constraint)
2. Try synonyms (e.g., "IT" vs. "technology")
3. Use Advanced Filters instead
4. Check if any tenders exist in that category (Dashboard)

#### Alerts Not Received

**Possible Causes**:
- Email notifications disabled in profile
- Email in spam folder
- No new tenders matching profile
- Alert threshold too high

**Solutions**:
1. Check vendor profile notification settings
2. Add BidRadar email to contacts
3. Check spam/junk folder
4. Lower match threshold to 40%
5. Verify profile has categories selected

### Performance Issues

**Slow Loading**:
- Large dataset (1000+ tenders): Use filters to narrow
- Slow internet: Wait for full page load before interacting
- Multiple browser tabs: Close unused tabs

**Page Not Responding**:
- Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser (Chrome recommended)

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is BidRadar free to use?**
A: Pricing depends on your deployment. This prototype is for evaluation purposes.

**Q: How often is tender data updated?**
A: Daily automated scraping. Manual refresh available on home page.

**Q: Which procurement portals are covered?**
A: Currently LUPC (London Universities Purchasing Consortium). More portals coming soon.

**Q: Can I access BidRadar on mobile?**
A: Yes, the interface is web-based and works on tablets and large phones. Desktop recommended for best experience.

### Data & Privacy

**Q: What data is stored about my company?**
A: Only the information you provide in your vendor profile (name, categories, preferences). No financial or proprietary information is required.

**Q: Is my data shared with other users or organizations?**
A: No. Your vendor profile is private and used only for your personalized recommendations.

**Q: Can I delete my data?**
A: Yes. Contact support or use the profile deletion option in Vendor Dashboard.

**Q: Where is tender data sourced from?**
A: Public procurement portals (currently LUPC). All data is publicly available information.

### Features & Functionality

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**
A: Typically 70-85% match accuracy for high-scoring recommendations (70%+). Accuracy improves as you mark interests.

**Q: Can I save searches?**
A: Search history is automatically saved. Export functionality available for specific searches.

**Q: Can multiple users from my company use the platform?**
A: Currently one vendor profile per company. Multi-user accounts in roadmap.

**Q: Can I track competitors?**
A: The platform shows market data and trends, but doesn't track specific competitor bids (this information isn't publicly available).

**Q: Does the AI actually read tender documents?**
A: The AI analyzes tender titles, descriptions, and metadata. Full PDF document analysis coming in future release.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome (recommended), Firefox, Safari, Edge. Internet Explorer not supported.

**Q: Do I need to install anything?**
A: No, it's fully web-based. Just need a modern browser and internet connection.

**Q: Can I integrate BidRadar with my CRM?**
A: Not directly yet. Use CSV export to import data into your CRM. API integration in roadmap.

**Q: What if I find a bug?**
A: Report bugs via the feedback form or contact support with details.
