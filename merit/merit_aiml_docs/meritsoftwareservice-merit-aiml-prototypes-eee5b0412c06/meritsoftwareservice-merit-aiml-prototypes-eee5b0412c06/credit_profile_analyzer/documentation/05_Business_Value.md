## Quantified Business Benefits

### 1. Time Savings Analysis

#### Traditional Credit Report Process

**Process Steps** (Manual):
1. Receive financial statements: 15 minutes
2. Extract data to spreadsheet: 60-90 minutes
3. Calculate financial ratios: 30-45 minutes
4. Analyze trends and benchmarks: 45-60 minutes
5. Write credit report narrative: 120-180 minutes
6. Review and formatting: 30-45 minutes
7. Quality control review: 30 minutes

**Total Time**: 5-7 hours per report
**Analyst Cost**: $75,000 annual salary = ~$40/hour
**Cost per Report**: $200-280 (direct labor only)

#### Automated Process with Credit Profile Analyzer

**Process Steps** (Automated):
1. Prepare CSV files: 10 minutes
2. Upload to system: 2 minutes
3. System processing: 2-3 minutes (AI generation)
4. Analyst review: 10-15 minutes
5. Final approval: 5 minutes

**Total Time**: 30 minutes per report
**Analyst Cost**: $40/hour × 0.5 hours = $20
**Software Cost**: $10,000/year ÷ 200 reports = $50/report
**Total Cost per Report**: $70

|--------|------------|-----------|-------------|

| Reports per Day | 1-2 | 15-20 | 10x increase |
| Reports per Month | 20 | 200 | 10x increase |
| Analyst Hours Saved | - | 90-130 hours/month | - |

- Traditional: 20 reports/month × 6 hours = 120 hours/month
