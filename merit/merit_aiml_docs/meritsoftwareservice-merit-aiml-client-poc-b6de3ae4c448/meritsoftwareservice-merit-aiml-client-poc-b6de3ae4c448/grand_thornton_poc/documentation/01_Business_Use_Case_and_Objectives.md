# Business Use Case and Objectives

## Executive Summary

The Grant Thornton POC (Proof of Concept) is an AI-powered financial analysis solution designed to automate the extraction of financial ratios and metrics from annual reports. This system addresses the critical need for accounting and audit firms to process large volumes of financial documents efficiently while maintaining accuracy and consistency.

## Business Problem

### Current Challenges in Financial Analysis

Financial analysis in accounting and audit firms faces several significant challenges:

1. **Time-Intensive Manual Extraction**: Analysts spend countless hours manually reviewing annual reports to extract financial metrics, ratios, and key performance indicators.

2. **Human Error Risk**: Manual extraction is prone to errors in transcription, calculation, and interpretation, potentially leading to incorrect financial assessments.

3. **Inconsistent Analysis**: Different analysts may interpret financial statements differently, leading to inconsistencies in extracted data and calculated ratios.

4. **Scalability Limitations**: Manual processes cannot efficiently scale to handle hundreds or thousands of annual reports during peak audit seasons.

5. **Delayed Insights**: The time required for manual analysis delays critical business decisions and client deliverables.

6. **High Operational Costs**: Significant labor costs are associated with manual financial data extraction and validation.

## Target Use Cases

### 1. Financial Statement Analysis

**Scenario**: Audit teams need to extract and analyze financial ratios from client annual reports to assess financial health and performance.

**Solution**: The POC automatically extracts key financial metrics from balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements, calculating over 50+ financial ratios instantly.

**Benefits**:
- Reduces analysis time from hours to minutes
- Ensures consistent extraction methodology
- Provides immediate ratio calculations
- Supports trend analysis across multiple years

### 2. Audit Support and Compliance

**Scenario**: Auditors require accurate financial data extraction to verify compliance with accounting standards and identify potential red flags.

**Solution**: The system extracts financial datapoints with source references and page numbers, enabling auditors to trace back to original documents for verification.

**Benefits**:
- Maintains audit trail with page number references
- Facilitates compliance verification
- Enables quick identification of anomalies
- Supports risk assessment processes

### 3. Due Diligence for M&A Transactions

**Scenario**: During mergers and acquisitions, teams need to analyze multiple companies' financial statements quickly to assess valuation and risk.

**Solution**: The POC can process multiple annual reports in parallel, extracting comparable financial metrics across different organizations.

**Benefits**:
- Accelerates due diligence timelines
- Enables comparative analysis across multiple entities
- Reduces risk of missing critical financial indicators
- Supports data-driven valuation decisions

### 4. Portfolio Performance Monitoring

**Scenario**: Investment teams and portfolio managers need regular updates on portfolio companies' financial performance.

**Solution**: Automated extraction of financial metrics enables continuous monitoring and trend analysis across portfolio companies.

**Benefits**:
- Real-time performance tracking
- Early warning system for financial deterioration
- Simplified regulatory reporting
- Enhanced client reporting capabilities

### 5. Credit Risk Assessment

**Scenario**: Lenders and financial institutions need to assess creditworthiness of borrowers based on their financial statements.

**Solution**: The system extracts and calculates key liquidity, leverage, and profitability ratios critical for credit risk evaluation.

**Benefits**:
- Standardized credit assessment process
- Faster loan processing decisions
- Consistent risk rating methodology
- Reduced credit approval cycle time
